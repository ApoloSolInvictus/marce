Gallery

Eterna Espressione was born from a passion. A passion to create, to express, and above all, to awaken emotions.

What specific emotion does this painting evoke? Have you ever felt this way - at peace, but restless at the same time? There is a moment, just before everything settles, when you still do not know whether you are about to fall apart or finally breathe. This painting is that moment. What do you see inside it? Answer me with one single word. The most beautiful answers will become the final title of this work. I do not paint to explain what I see. I paint to give shape to what I feel. That is why I will never tell you what a work means. The title is mine. The rest - what comforts you, what unsettles you, what reminds you of something you thought you had forgotten - that is yours. As in the kitchen, where each dish becomes complete only when someone tastes it, in painting too the artwork is completed only before the one who looks at it. I have done my part. Now it is your turn. This is a unique work. When it is sold, it will exist nowhere else in the world. I am not looking for someone who understands my art. I am looking for someone who, when looking at it, understands something about themselves. Take it with you. It is not just another painting - it is a piece of you that you did not know you were searching for. If it stopped you for more than three seconds, perhaps it was not by chance. Some works are looked at. This one is taken home. Make it yours - purchase link in bio.
